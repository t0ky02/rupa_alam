The beginning

Not a business plan.
A personal ritual.

At the end of the day, there is a moment when the noise begins to disappear. Work is finished. The body slows down. The mind becomes quieter.

For me, this moment often came at night, surrounded by nature, looking toward the lemongrass growing outside, and taking time to care for my skin with botanical oils.

It was something simple. Oil warmed between the palms. The fragrance of plants rising gently. A few quiet minutes to massage the skin. The scent of flowers, leaves and woods lingering in the air.

Over time I realized that what I valued was not simply the effect of an oil on my skin. I valued the ritual itself. It was a moment of returning to myself.

From that night came body-perfumery oils — and the wish for a house where other people could receive the same evening.

Rupa means form. Alam means nature. Together they are how beauty looks when it stays close to the earth. The craft is studied with the discipline of perfume. The plants are Indonesian. The climate is humid, so the oils stay light.
